Development

Following the necessary research, our foundation invests in developing tools to empower people. We provide means for long-term resiliency, so our communities are able improve their skills, acquire the resources they need to build a life they intimately care for.

Once our solutions are complete and sustainably maintained, countless people may use them, multiplying the benefits of our investment. While we provide the education for the tools we develop and distribute, we can also train various skill sets for self-sufficiency.

Accountability is integral for all our development work, setting goals in line with our objectives, documenting our consultations and decisions on the way. We understand that it is critical that we allocate our time and resources where we have the most mindful and meaningful impact, and evaluate our progress by objective and measurable standards.
